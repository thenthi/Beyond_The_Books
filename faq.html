FAQ

Got questions? I've got answers!

What is a Living Book?

A Living Book is a real person who shares their story, experiences or passion with you. Instead of reading a book, you sit down and have a conversation.

How do I book a session?

Go to Booking, pick a topic and a Living Book, choose a date and time, then leave your name and email. We'll send you a confirmation email.

What can I ask?

Anything about the Living Book's story, work or interests. Please be respectful and avoid overly personal questions. See the rules on the home page.

Can I cancel or change my booking?

Yes. Just reply to your confirmation email and let us know, so the time slot can be opened for someone else.

How can I become a Living Book?

We'd love to hear your story! Fill in the Be a Living Book form and we'll get in touch.
