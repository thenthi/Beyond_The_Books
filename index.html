About us

Beyond the Book is a project that brings people together through stories, experiences, and conversations.

Instead of borrowing a traditional book, you can meet a Living Book and join a one on one or small group conversation. Each Living Book shares their own experiences, interests, or perspectives, giving participants the opportunity to ask questions, exchange ideas, and learn something new from another person.

Beyond the Book aims to create opportunities for connection, helping people feel less alone while discovering the stories and experiences that exist beyond the pages of a book.

FAQ

What is a Living Book?

A Living Book is a real person who shares their own experiences, interests, or perspectives in a conversation with you.

Is it one on one or a group?

Both. You can join a one on one chat or a small group conversation, depending on the Living Book.

How long is a session?

Each conversation lasts about 30 minutes.

Does it cost anything?

No, it's free.
